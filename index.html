What this receptionist can do

A real version would be customized using the clinic’s real services, approved answers, calendar, and brand voice.

Answer common questions It can answer questions about services, hours, booking, and clinic policies.
Collect interested leads It asks for the visitor’s name and contact information.
Guide people to book It can send visitors to a clinic’s booking calendar.
Send hard questions to staff When the question needs a real person, it asks the team to follow up.

How it works

  1. A person visits the clinic website.
  2. They ask a question in the chat.
  3. The assistant responds immediately.
  4. The person books or leaves contact details.
  5. The clinic team sees the lead in one place.
